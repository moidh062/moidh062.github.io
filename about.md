I am a Biomedical Engineering graduate from the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), with a focus on deep learning, medical imaging, and clinical decision support systems for low-resource settings. My goal is to develop interpretable and generalizable AI methodologies that seamlessly integrate into clinical practice. As an MS/PhD aspirant for Fall 2026, I aim to advance healthcare technology through innovative research.

My undergraduate thesis centered on explainable deep learning models for tuberculosis detection from chest radiographs. Utilizing knowledge distillation, I achieved robust disease localization without manual annotations, enhancing cross-dataset performance. This work reflects my commitment to creating accurate, interpretable AI solutions for clinical applications. 
My projects include View-Retina, a cost-effective smartphone ophthalmoscope employing image processing for early glaucoma detection, and a deep learning-based diabetic retinopathy classification system deployed via a web application. Additionally, I co-founded PakhiDroneBD, an AI-driven agro-drone initiative to optimize pesticide use, demonstrating interdisciplinary innovation.

Beyond academics, I held communication roles in BUET Animata and the BUET BMES Student Chapter, and contributed to event management for the Bangladesh Biology Olympiad and BUET ACS Student Chapter. These experiences strengthened my leadership and organizational skills, complementing my technical expertise.

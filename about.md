<p>
  I am a <strong>Biomedical Engineering graduate</strong> from the <strong>Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET)</strong>, with a focus on <strong>deep learning</strong>, <strong>medical imaging</strong>, and <strong>clinical decision support systems</strong> for low-resource settings. My goal is to develop <strong>interpretable and generalizable AI methodologies</strong> that seamlessly integrate into clinical practice. As an <strong>MS/PhD aspirant for Fall 2026</strong>, I aim to advance healthcare technology through innovative research.
</p>

<p>
  My undergraduate thesis centered on <strong>explainable deep learning models for tuberculosis detection</strong> from chest radiographs. Utilizing <strong>knowledge distillation</strong>, I achieved robust disease localization without manual annotations, enhancing cross-dataset performance. This work reflects my commitment to creating <strong>accurate, interpretable AI solutions</strong> for clinical applications.
</p>

<p>
  My projects include <strong>View-Retina</strong>, a cost-effective smartphone ophthalmoscope employing image processing for early <strong>glaucoma detection</strong>, and a <strong>deep learning-based diabetic retinopathy classification system</strong> deployed via a web application. Additionally, I co-founded <strong>PakhiDroneBD</strong>, an <strong>AI-driven agro-drone initiative</strong> to optimize pesticide use, demonstrating interdisciplinary innovation.
</p>

<p>
  Beyond academics, I held communication roles in <strong>BUET Animata</strong> and the <strong>BUET BMES Student Chapter</strong>, and contributed to event management for the <strong>Bangladesh Biology Olympiad</strong> and <strong>BUET ACS Student Chapter</strong>. These experiences strengthened my <strong>leadership and organizational skills</strong>, complementing my technical expertise.
</p>
